## Scope and constraints

This batch is the first real-prospect research batch following the CAL-01–CAL-16 calibration and the
CAL-12 controlled validation of the rationale reconciliation fix.

- No scores, routes, thresholds, or outreach recommendations are assigned in this document. Product-led
  (P1–P8) and company-fit (A1–A15) observations are recorded as **evidence**, not as ratings.
- Purchase Readiness is recorded **only** where a source supplies an explicit dated event that still falls
  inside its decay window. It is never inferred from title, seniority, or strategic relevance.
- Three of ten prospects carry no surviving readiness signal. Each records that fact explicitly rather than
  substituting a plausible-sounding one.
- No outreach drafted, no messages sent, no meetings created.

## Method

Every record traces to a source that was opened and read, not to a search-result summary. Where a summary
and the primary source disagreed, the primary source won — this changed the outcome for PR-07, where a
widely repeated "2026" date is in fact 2025-07-24.

A search for private-equity value-creation forcing events returned only generic trend commentary with no
named prospects. Nothing was taken from it. No evidence anywhere in this batch is invented.

## Review notes and open questions

1. **Apollo account-linkage defect (corrected for ingestion, preserved as evidence).** Michael Galtman
   (CON-0034) and Melissa Barnes (CON-0035) are both filed under "Rockwell Automation, Inc." while their notes
   describe Chesapeake Utilities. The account-name column appears misaligned across the Contacts sheet. The
   corrected employer is used for ingestion so no knowingly incorrect relationship enters Dataverse; the
   original discrepancy is preserved above and in the PR-02 evidence record. The source workbook still needs
   fixing — this batch corrects the record, not the sheet.
2. **`_evidence_supports_signal_date()` was fixed and deployed before this batch loaded (2026-08-24).** The
   previous guardrail returned `True` unconditionally when `Evidence.observed_at` was set, bypassing the
   requirement that the readiness date appear in the evidence claim. A first fix that let `observed_at`
   corroborate a same-day signal was rejected during live probing: with no date to work from, the model simply
   echoed `observed_at` back, turning "took office last spring" into a signal dated that day. The shipped rule
   is therefore claim-text only - `observed_at` records when evidence was observed, not when an event occurred,
   so it plays no part in date attestation. Deployed as Function v0.0.12; 19 local tests pass and both live
   probes behave correctly.

3. **Dataverse field lengths were extended on 2026-08-24 and the workaround unwound.** `new_sourceurl` went
   from 100 to 1000 characters; `new_whynow`, `new_selectionrationale` and `new_researchsummary` from 100 to
   4000. All existing data was preserved - increasing MaxLength is non-destructive - and customisations were
   published. The eight PR evidence rows that had carried their URL inside `new_claim` now store it in
   `new_sourceurl`, with the claim text otherwise unchanged, and the ten prospect records now hold their full
   researched `new_whynow` and `new_selectionrationale` text rather than the 100-character summaries the old
   cap forced.

   The CAL-12 truncated URL was **repaired**, not reconstructed: the complete URL was recovered from the Jaspen
   Sales Operating System workbook, which is the authoritative source record for that evidence, and the stored
   100-character value was verified to be an exact prefix of it before writing. Both CAL-12 evidence rows
   carried the same truncated URL and both were corrected.
4. **Public sector.** PR-05 and PR-09 have two of the strongest decision shapes in the batch but carry
   procurement constraints that may make them poor uses of human sales capacity whatever the engine scores.
5. **A15 proximity.** PR-08 and PR-10 sit near the hard gate. Both are retained with the concern recorded so
   the engine applies the gate; neither was excluded by research judgment.
6. **Month-precision dates.** PR-08 and PR-10 have month-only source dates. Expect Purchase Readiness 0 on
   both if the guardrail requires full date precision. That is correct behaviour, not a defect.

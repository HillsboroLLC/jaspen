# Key Messages

## One-Sentence Positioning

Jaspen is a Decision Intelligence platform that turns messy context into explainable, confidence-calibrated decisions through AI conversation, user-owned rubrics, deterministic scoring, evidence grading, transparent reasoning, and execution planning, with canonical Decision Records forming the foundation for future organizational learning.

## Five-Second Explanation

Jaspen is not an AI chatbot. It is how you make the call you can defend.

## Thirty-Second Explanation

Jaspen helps people and organizations turn messy context into structured decisions they can explain and act on. AI helps interview the user and judge evidence, but the scoring is deterministic, the rubric belongs to the user, and confidence is tied to evidence. The output is the decision artifact, and the canonical Decision Record foundation preserves that reasoning over time. General AI gives you an answer. Jaspen gives you a decision you can explain and defend when someone asks why it was a 72.

## Investor Explanation

Jaspen is re-founding Decision Intelligence — a category analysts named but first built as data-science tooling — as a constitutional, human-governed architecture between AI conversation, analytics, workflow, and organizational knowledge. General AI assistants produce answers. Jaspen produces structured decision artifacts and is formalizing canonical Decision Records as private, customer-owned organizational memory by default. Its architecture separates human judgment, AI evidence judgment, and deterministic scoring — a trust model enterprises can understand. The moat compounds: outcome history accumulates at the speed of reality and cannot be synthesized, and an organization's decision memory living in its records is a switching cost that feels like loyalty.

## CIO Explanation

Jaspen is designed for AI-assisted decision support with governance built in. The user owns the rubric, code performs scoring calculations, evidence confidence caps unsupported conclusions, and canonical Decision Records preserve the reasoning behind decisions. Custody rings distinguish private customer records, explicitly opted-in and anonymized public library records, and governed non-identifying internal evaluation/calibration use. Jaspen does not eliminate AI hallucinations. It is designed to resist unsupported conclusions through constitutional constraints, deterministic scoring, evidence grading, confidence caps, user-owned rubrics, labeled assumptions, and transparent reasoning.

## CEO Explanation

Jaspen helps leadership teams make decisions that are clearer, faster, and easier to defend. It turns scattered context into criteria, weights, evidence, assumptions, confidence, recommendation, and execution plan. Over time, canonical Decision Records can become private organizational memory, helping the company learn which assumptions fail, which evidence matters, and how decision quality improves. Jaspen does not replace leadership judgment. It gives leadership judgment structure and memory.

## Founder Explanation

Jaspen gives founders a structured decision partner when they do not have a full executive team around every choice. It helps clarify the decision, compare alternatives, expose trade-offs, score with honest confidence, and turn the choice into an execution plan. The long-term value is a record of how the company made its decisions: what mattered, what was assumed, what worked, and what should change next time.

# Value Proposition

Jaspen's value changes by customer altitude, but the core promise is consistent: turn uncertain context into explainable, confidence-calibrated decisions that can be acted on and learned from.

## Individuals

### Problems Solved

Individuals face high-stakes choices with incomplete information: job offers, career moves, relocation, education, purchases, health-adjacent planning, financial trade-offs, and life decisions where priorities are hard to compare.

The problem is rarely lack of advice. It is lack of structure.

### ROI

For an individual, ROI is clarity, reduced regret, faster movement, and a better record of why the decision made sense at the time.

### Why Choose Jaspen

Jaspen helps the individual define criteria, weigh trade-offs, see assumptions, compare alternatives, and move from rumination to action. It can provide a first score with honest confidence even when evidence is incomplete. (The canonical example threaded through this documentation — a job offer weighed against staying — is exactly this altitude.)

### Long-Term Value

Over time, personal Decision Records can become a private library of judgment: what mattered, what changed, what worked, and what the person learned about their own priorities.

## Solopreneurs

### Problems Solved

Solopreneurs make decisions without a full leadership team: pricing, offers, positioning, hiring contractors, choosing markets, prioritizing products, and deciding what not to do.

The problem is too many trade-offs and too few trusted mirrors.

### ROI

ROI shows up as faster prioritization, fewer scattered bets, better use of limited time and cash, and clearer confidence about which assumptions need evidence.

### Why Choose Jaspen

Jaspen gives a solo operator a decision process that feels like a structured advisor: criteria, evidence, trade-offs, scoring, recommendation, and execution plan in one flow.

### Long-Term Value

Decision Records can become the operating memory of the business. The solopreneur can see how decisions evolved from intuition to evidence and where repeated patterns may emerge.

## Small Businesses

### Problems Solved

Small businesses often make important decisions through owner instinct, team opinions, spreadsheets, and urgency. Choices about hiring, vendors, capital spend, marketing, operations, and customer focus can become scattered.

The problem is not that small businesses lack judgment. It is that judgment is not always captured, compared, or revisited.

### ROI

ROI comes from better prioritization, fewer bad investments, faster alignment, clearer execution, and decisions that survive beyond a single conversation.

### Why Choose Jaspen

Jaspen offers structure without enterprise overhead. Teams can use a shared rubric, see the evidence behind a score, expose assumptions, and turn the decision into work.

### Long-Term Value

The business builds a memory of what it chose and why. New team members can understand prior reasoning instead of inheriting folklore.

## Mid-Market

### Problems Solved

Mid-market organizations have enough scale for decisions to become cross-functional, but often lack the governance maturity of large enterprises. Initiatives compete for resources, departments use different criteria, and executive alignment can arrive late.

The problem is portfolio quality: which decisions deserve resources, in what order, and with what confidence.

### ROI

ROI appears in improved capital allocation, fewer stalled initiatives, faster cross-functional alignment, better resource sequencing, and more transparent executive review.

### Why Choose Jaspen

Jaspen gives mid-market teams a repeatable decision language: user-owned rubrics, deterministic scoring, evidence grading, confidence calibration, and execution planning. It helps teams defend recommendations without turning every choice into a deck-building exercise.

### Long-Term Value

Decision Records can become a mid-market operating asset. Over time, patterns across initiatives may reveal recurring bottlenecks, weak assumptions, and which evidence most improves outcomes.

## Enterprise

### Problems Solved

Enterprises struggle with decision fragmentation: many teams, many frameworks, many systems, many stakeholders, and limited institutional memory. Decisions are made in decks, meetings, documents, workflows, and conversations that rarely become a unified learning system.

The problem is not intelligence. It is decision architecture.

### ROI

ROI includes better governance, more defensible prioritization, improved capital allocation, faster executive alignment, stronger auditability, more consistent rubric use, and the foundation for a growing institutional memory of decisions and outcomes.

### Why Choose Jaspen

Jaspen is designed for trust: human-owned criteria, deterministic scoring, evidence grading, confidence caps, transparent reasoning, private-by-default Decision Records, and custody rings for governed future learning. It can help enterprises standardize decision quality without centralizing all judgment.

### Long-Term Value

The long-term enterprise path is a governed Decision Library and internal evaluation/calibration corpus that compound only under clear custody rules. Over time, the organization may identify decision patterns, calibrate confidence, improve governance, onboard leaders faster, and make institutional judgment less dependent on memory, politics, or individual tenure.
